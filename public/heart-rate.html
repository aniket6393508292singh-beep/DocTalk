Heart Rate Guide

Simple instructions to measure your pulse and a small tap-based counter to estimate beats per minute.

How to check your pulse

  1. Find your wrist (radial) or neck (carotid).
  2. Use two fingers (index and middle) and press lightly.
  3. Count beats for 15 seconds and multiply by 4 to get BPM.

Quick Tap Counter

Tap the button once per beat for a short period, then press Calculate.
