Nutrition & Diet Tips

Basic, evidence-aligned advice for everyday healthy eating.

Core Tips

  • Eat a variety of vegetables and fruits — aim for color and seasonality.
  • Prioritize whole grains and minimally processed foods.
  • Include lean protein at meals (legumes, fish, poultry, tofu).
  • Prefer healthy fats (olive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado) in moderation.
  • Stay hydrated — water is best. Limit sugary drinks.

Simple Meal Ideas

  • Overnight oats with fruit and a sprinkle of nuts.
  • Mixed salad with chickpeas, whole-grain bread, and olive oil.
  • Grilled fish with steamed vegetables and brown rice.

When to seek personalised advice

If you have chronic conditions (diabetes, kidney disease, severe allergies) consult a registered dietitian or your doctor before major diet changes.
