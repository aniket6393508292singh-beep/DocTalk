Essential First Aid Medicines & Supplies

These items are commonly used for immediate at-home first aid. Follow instructions and seek medical care for serious injuries.

Paracetamol (Acetaminophen) Use: Pain relief, fever reducer Dosage: Adults 500–1000 mg every 4–6 hours (max 3 g/day). Check pediatric dosing for children.
Ibuprofen Use: Anti-inflammatory, pain relief Dosage: Adults 200–400 mg every 4–6 hours (max 1200 mg/day OTC). Avoid if gastric ulcer or kidney disease.
Antiseptic Spray / Solution Use: Clean wounds, prevent infection Apply to cleaned wound area; avoid deep puncture wounds—seek professional care.
Adhesive Bandages & Gauze Use: Cover and protect cuts or abrasions Choose appropriate size; change dressings daily or if soiled.
Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) Use: Rehydrate in case of diarrhea/vomiting Mix with clean water as instructed on the packet. Give small sips frequently.
Antihistamine (Cetirizine) Use: Allergic reactions, hives Dosage: Adults 10 mg once daily. Seek emergency care for breathing difficulty.
Burn Gel / Hydrogel Use: Cool and soothe minor burns Apply gently; for severe burns seek emergency care.
