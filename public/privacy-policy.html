Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

About This Website

DockTalk is a college project and frontend demonstration of a telemedicine management platform. This website is not a real medical provider and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or emergency services.

Information Used in the Demo

The website may temporarily use information entered into forms, such as a name, email address, appointment details, symptoms, height, weight, and chat messages. In this prototype, this information is used only to demonstrate interface behavior.

No information is sent to a real doctor, laboratory, pharmacy, payment provider, or server from this frontend prototype.

Browser Storage

The demo uses your browser's localStorage to keep sample account status, shopping-cart items, and order history on the current device. This data can be removed by clearing the website's browser storage.

Do not enter real passwords, medical records, payment details, or other sensitive information into this academic prototype.

Health and Emergency Information

Health calculators, symptom guidance, first-aid instructions, wellness articles, and sample doctor profiles are provided for educational demonstration only. They are not medical diagnoses or treatment instructions.

For an emergency, contact your local emergency services or visit the nearest hospital. Do not rely on this website for urgent care.

Third-Party Content

Some sample images and article imagery may be loaded from external image services. Those services may process requests according to their own privacy policies. The project does not control or manage those services.

Security and Changes

This project does not provide production-grade authentication, encryption, access control, or server-side data protection. The policy may be updated if the project is extended with a backend or real service integrations.

Contact

For academic questions about this project, contact the student developer or project guide listed in the accompanying project report.
